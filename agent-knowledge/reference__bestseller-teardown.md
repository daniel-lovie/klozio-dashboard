## ⚠️ The $6.99 illusion — how the price display works

Displayed on the listing: **"Now $6.99+  ~~$11.65+~~  40% off · Sale ends in 10:09:26"**

The size dropdown shows what a buyer actually pays:

| Variation | Price |
|---|---|
| Comfort Colors S / M / L / XL | **$21.98** |
| Comfort Colors 2XL | $24.98 |
| Comfort Colors 3XL | $27.98 |
| Comfort Colors 4XL | $30.38 |
| Youth Comfort S / M / L | $21.98 |
| **Custom Order Fee** | **$6.99** ← the displayed price |

**Mechanism:** Etsy shows a listing's price as the **cheapest variation**, with a `+`. By adding a
non-garment variation ("Custom Order Fee") at $6.99, the listing advertises "$6.99+" while every actual
shirt is $21.98. The `+` is technically honest and easy to miss.

Effects they get:
- Appears in low-price filters and sorts
- Massive perceived-value gap in the grid vs competitors showing $16–22
- Combined with a permanent "40% off" + countdown for a second discount signal

**Honest read before we copy this:**
- It is a **grey-hat display tactic.** Etsy prohibits misleading pricing, and a $6.99 "size option" that
  isn't a product is exactly the kind of thing that gets re-interpreted during a policy sweep.
- The permanent countdown sale contradicts our own research: *"Avoid permanent sales — trains buyers to
  wait."* Every competitor ad in the same screenshot runs it ($9.60 was $16.00, $9.00 was $15.00,
  $9.33 was $13.33…), so the whole category has trained its buyers already.
- **Recommendation: do not add a fake cheap variation.** Do consider a genuine sale price and honest
  size-based upcharges, which is most of the perceived-value effect without the policy exposure.

Decision is the user's; this is documented so it is a choice and not an accident.

## Cover image — the highest-value lesson

Their cover is a **styled flat lay**, not a bare mockup:

- Folded shirt, front panel flat, shot slightly overhead on a white/very light surface
- **Props framing the shirt:** tan felt cowboy hat (top-left), dried palm frond (right), a fine
  silver necklace draped over the collar
- **The Comfort Colors neck label is deliberately visible** — it proves the blank brand, which the title
  also sells. Strong trust signal.
- **Colorway name written in the top-right corner** in a casual script: *"Espresso"*
- Design is **large, colorful, high-detail** and occupies most of the chest
- Warm, cohesive, editorial colour grade

Note: the corner colorway text **contradicts our own "no text overlay on slot 1" rule.** Etsy's guidance
says no text overlay; category bestsellers put the colorway name there anyway and it clearly is not
hurting them. Treat it as a small, single-word, corner-placed exception — not a licence for badges,
starbursts, or "SALE" overlays.

## Carousel

The thumbnail rail shows **~12 images, one per colorway**, each a flat lay in the same styling with the

## Title

```
Comfort Colors® She's From Texas Shirt, Retro Texas T-Shirt, Texas Girl Tee,
Southern State Pride Gift, Texas Lover Gift
```

- **Leads with `Comfort Colors®`** — brand-first, including the ® symbol
- **Five** comma-separated phrases
- ~115 characters — longer than the 80–95 band we held when this teardown was written
- Two of five phrases are gift framings ("Southern State Pride Gift", "Texas Lover Gift")

This was the counter-evidence that retired the band. The 80–95 figure came from reconciling two
conflicting internal sources, not from market observation; a 6.19%-converting bestseller at ~115 chars
outweighed it, and the operating band was set to **125–140** on 2026-08-14 — above this listing, on the
reasoning that if 115 beats 95 there is no argument for leaving the rest of the field empty.

## Description structure

Long, heavily sectioned with `---------- Section ----------` dividers:

1. **How to Order Your Custom Design T-shirt** — bulleted steps (choose colour, size, design/text colour)
2. **Product** — hard garment specs: *4.2 oz, 100% airlume combed and ringspun cotton, 32 singles;
   Athletic/Black Heather 90/10; Ash 99/1; double-needle stitching; seamless collar; heat transfer label;
   taped shoulder-to-shoulder*
3. **Production and Shipping** — *Production: 1–2 days*; standard shipping 3–5 business days after
   production; expedited upgrade available; rush fee on request
4. Operational caveats — colour-chart warnings, *"we have three different t-shirt brands that we use"*,
   wrong-address policy, business hours (9am–6pm CST, Mon–Fri)

Two observations:
- **Specs sell.** Concrete garment numbers do trust work that adjectives cannot.
- They **hedge the blank** ("three different brands") while the title says Comfort Colors®. We sell
  genuine Comfort Colors, so we can state it without hedging — that is a real differentiator we should
  use rather than imitate.

## 🚨 What NOT to copy — the design is blatantly infringing

The artwork is a maximalist collage of: a **Dr Pepper** bottle, the **H-E-B** logo, the **Buc-ee's** beaver
logo, a **Whataburger** cup, cowboy boots, bluebonnets, a cowboy hat, a Texas state charm, the Texas flag,
and a city skyline.

**Dr Pepper, H-E-B, Buc-ee's and Whataburger are all registered trademarks.** This listing has no
plausible licence. A 2-month-old shop with this catalogue is a takedown candidate, and per
`etsy-tshirt-research/references/ip-incident-response.md` strikes are shop-level, not listing-level.

**Learn the style, never the marks:**

| Copy this | Never this |
|---|---|
| Maximalist, colourful, high-detail collage | Real brand logos or product likenesses |
| Local/regional pride as the identity hook | Trademarked slogans |
| Charm-bracelet / sticker-sheet composition | Franchise or team marks |
| Warm nostalgic palette | Celebrity likenesses |
| Recognisable regional *symbols* (bluebonnets, boots, state outline) | Recognisable regional *companies* |

The same composition built from generic regional symbols carries the same appeal with none of the exposure.
